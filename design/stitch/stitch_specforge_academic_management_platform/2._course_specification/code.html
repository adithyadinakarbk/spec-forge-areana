Module-Wise Syllabus & Textbooks

Breakdown of pedagogical units, scheduled lecture hours, laboratory exercises, and standard references.

42 Lecture Hours Total
Module 1: Stacks, Queues & Deques 8 Lecture Hrs

Array & singly/doubly linked implementations; circular queues; amortized analysis; applications: infix-to-postfix translation, recursion unrolling, depth-limited search buffers.

Module 2: Trees & Disjoint Sets 10 Lecture Hrs

Binary Search Trees; AVL tree rotations; Red-Black node balance criteria; B-Trees indexing fundamentals; Union-Find disjoint sets with path compression and rank union.

Module 3: Graph Algorithms & Minimum Spanning Forests 12 Lecture Hrs

Adjacency matrices vs. lists; BFS, DFS, and topological sort; Prim's and Kruskal's MST algorithms; Dijkstra and Bellman-Ford shortest paths; Strongly Connected Components (Tarjan/Kosaraju).

Module 4: Hashing, Heaps & Amortized Data Structures 12 Lecture Hrs

Binary heaps, Fibonacci heaps, universal hashing families, cryptographic collision resistance principles, bloom filters, and skip lists.

terminal Laboratory Practicum Suite (28 Contact Hrs)
12 Hands-on Exercises + Mini-Project

Includes comprehensive Valgrind memory leak profiling, custom allocator implementations in modern C++, benchmark automation, and graph visualizers.

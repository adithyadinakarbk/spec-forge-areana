4. Module-Wise Syllabus & Laboratory Components

Total Contact: 42 Lecture + 28 Lab Hrs
Module 1

Foundations of Distributed Architectures

8 Lecture Hours | 19% Weightage

Characterization of distributed systems; Design goals, challenges, and architectural styles (Layered, Object-based, Event-based); Communication models: Remote Procedure Calls (gRPC), Message-Oriented Middleware; Asynchronous messaging semantics.

menu_book Ref: Tanenbaum & Van Steen (Ch. 1–3)
Module 2

Time, Coordination & Global State

9 Lecture Hours | 22% Weightage

Physical and logical clock synchronization: Christian's algorithm, Berkeley algorithm, Lamport timestamps, Vector clocks; Distributed mutual exclusion (Ricart-Agrawala, Token Ring); Global snapshot capture (Chandy-Lamport algorithm); Leader election (Bully and Ring algorithms).

menu_book Ref: Coulouris et al. (Ch. 14–15)
Module 3

Consensus, Fault Tolerance & Replication

10 Lecture Hours | 24% Weightage

The FLP Impossibility Theorem; Failure detectors; Two-Phase Commit (2PC) and Three-Phase Commit (3PC); Paxos algorithm; Raft consensus: Leader election, Log replication, and Safety proofs; Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT introduction).

menu_book Ref: Ongaro & Ousterhout (Raft Paper, 2014)
terminal

Hands-on Laboratory Suite

  • Implementation of distributed key-value store using gRPC & Go.
  • Simulation of Lamport clocks and vector clock causal ordering.
  • Deploying and stress-testing a 3-node Raft consensus cluster.
  • Containerized cloud deployment on localized Kubernetes cluster.
